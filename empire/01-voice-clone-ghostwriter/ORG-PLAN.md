# ORG PLAN — 01 Voice-Clone Ghostwriter (org: solo SaaS + agency hybrid)
Mission: give every founder a $5k/mo ghostwriter's brain for the price of lunch.

## Placement (which shelf, between whom)
Shelf: X growth tools, between Tweet Hunter ($99/mo, dashboard chore) and human ghostwriters ($3–5k/mo, our anchor). We sit UNDER the human on price, ABOVE the tools on outcome: not "write tweets" but "be my head of content." Left neighbor to avoid: generic AI writers (slop reputation). Stand next to Stanley-for-X proof ($4k MRR in 48h at $30–149) — same shelf, SMS wedge differentiates.

## Pricing architecture
- Starter $49: 1 voice, X only, 30 posts/mo. Tripwire.
- Pro $99 (hero, expect 60% mix): X + LinkedIn, unlimited drafts, weekly voice retrain.
- Agency $149: 3 voices (founder + company + personal), approval inbox for VAs.
- DFY onboarding $500 once: we build your voice profile from calls/transcripts in 48h (funds CAC, commits the buyer).
Math: 60 subs at ~$89 blended = $5.3k MRR. Churn guard: voice compounds — month-3 output visibly better than month-1; show the diff in-app ("your voice match: 91%").

## Packaging
SMS-first (text an idea, get a thread), weekly digest email of scheduled posts, voice-match score card. No dashboard to learn — that's the package.

## Channels (ordered)
1. Build in public on founder's own X (dogfood = demo). 2. Free PDF case study for RT (Stanley playbook). 3. Product Hunt. 4. SEO: "AI head of content", "Twitter ghostwriter alternative".

## 30-60-90 + kill
D30: 10 DFY pilots at $150 (validates willingness + trains voices). D60: 30 paying subs or pivot niche (agencies? coaches?). D90: 60 subs / $5k MRR or kill SaaS, keep DFY agency.
Needs: OpenAI key, Twilio, founder's own transcripts (NEEDS.md).
