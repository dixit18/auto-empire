# 09 - Wellness Audio (Wellness / Mental Health)
Industry: Wellness | Model: $9-19 packs + $7-29/mo | Target: $1500/mo passive

## Business
Problem: Generic meditation saturated. Specific anxieties underserved, high repeat use.
Solution: Audio packs for narrow avatars: ADHD moms sleep stories (7-min), founders focus loops (25-min), PCOS anxiety relief. + printable gratitude/sleep tracker bundle ($10-15).
Proof: Audio low competition, high loyalty. Self-care for moms 32/40. Niche eBook $3200/60d zero ads.
ICP: Moms 25-44 Pinterest/TikTok, founders burnt out.
Math: 100 x $12 = $1200 + 50 x $19/mo = $950. 90%+ margin, Gumroad instant delivery.
Moat: Voice + ritual (daily habit) + bundle with printables (#05 cross-sell).

## TEAM
**MASTER: Wellness Curator**
Prompt: "You are calm, safe, specific. Coordinate Writer, Voicer, Bundler. No medical claims. Cozy, repeatable."

**A1 - ScriptWriter:** 800-word scripts, breathing cues, no jargon. Variants: sleep, focus, wind-down.
**A2 - Voicer:** ElevenLabs warm voice + brown noise mix. 7/15/25-min cuts.
**A3 - Bundler:** Pairs audio + habit tracker printable + Notion log. Gumroad page with lifestyle mockup.
**A4 - Nurturer:** Email 7-day challenge ($47 front-end) -> $19/mo group continuity.

## PHASES
P0: Validate 1 avatar (ADHD moms). 5 interviews, 1 free track for 20 emails.
P1: Ship 10-track pack $12. TikTok "Result vs Ritual" videos.
P2: Add membership $19/mo (new drop weekly) + challenge funnel.
P3: License to coaches as white-label "between-session continuity" $77-197/mo (links to #06).

KPIs: Listen completion, repeat 7-day %, refund <10%, MRR.
First $100: Pre-sell 10 packs in mom Discord.
