# TRACK 01 "Shutdown" — full narration (7 min, ADHD moms)
Voice: slow, warm. Pauses written in. Brown-noise bed under, 60s fade.

Welcome. The day is done, even if your mind hasn't heard the news yet. Let's fix that, together. ... Get comfortable. ... Really comfortable. Move once more, ... and then be still.

We're going to breathe. In through your nose for four. ... Two. ... Three. ... Four. ... Hold, just a moment. ... And out, slow, for seven. ... Two. ... Three. ... Four. ... Five. ... Six. ... Seven. ... Good. Again. In. ... Two. ... Three. ... Four. ... And out. ... Let your shoulders drop as the air leaves. ... One more. In. ... And out. ... Good.

Now. Shoulders. You've been carrying them near your ears all day. Lift them, up, up, ... and let them fall. ... Feel that? Do it once more. Lift. ... Drop. ... Good. ... Your jaw. Unclench it. Let your teeth part, just a little. ... Let your tongue rest heavy. ... Your hands. Open them. Spread your fingers wide, ... and let them curl closed, soft, like you're holding something small and warm. ... Good.

Now picture the kitchen. Or the living room. Or wherever the mess lives tonight. ... See it. ... The cups. The crumbs. The small shoes in the hallway. ... Now watch this. The cups stack themselves. ... The crumbs sweep themselves into a neat little line, ... and off the edge. ... The shoes pair up and walk, very politely, to the door. ... Everything folding itself away. ... You don't have to do any of it. ... It's doing itself. ... Watch until it's done. ... There. ... Quiet room. ... Nothing needs you.

The list. I know there's a list. ... Bills. Messages. That thing for school. ... Take each one, gently, and put it in the drawer. ... Open the drawer. ... First worry, in. ... Second, in. ... Third. ... In. ... Close the drawer. ... It will all be there in the morning, exactly where you left it. ... Nothing spoils overnight. ... The drawer holds. ... You rest.

Think of one small thing from today that was... fine. ... Warm water on your hands. ... The way the light came in, for a minute. ... A sound your kid made that was almost a laugh. ... Hold just that one. ... Nothing else. ... Just that.

Your body is getting heavier now. ... Legs heavy. ... Arms heavy. ... Eyelids heavy. ... The brown hum underneath you is carrying a little of your weight. ... Let it take a little more. ... And a little more. ...

The list waits till morning. ... The room is quiet. ... The drawer is closed. ... You did enough today. ... More than enough. ... Sleep now. ...
