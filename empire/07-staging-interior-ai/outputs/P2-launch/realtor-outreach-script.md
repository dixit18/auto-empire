# Compound rev — realtor outreach script (Lister → Lead)
Date: 2026-09-08. Offer: 3 free staged photos for 1 listing. No call required.

DM (Facebook realtor groups, <60 words):
"Hi [Name] — staged your [Street] listing 3 ways in 30 seconds so you can see it: [before/after link]. First 3 photos free, $29/mo after that if your sellers like them. Want me to do the other rooms?"

Follow-up (48h, one line): "Keeping your 3 free stages open till Friday — want the living room too?"

Objections:
- "AI looks fake" → "That's why the first 3 are free — judge the photos, not the tech."
- "My photographer stages" → "Keep them. We cover the listings between shoots and the stale ones at day 30."
- "Send pricing" → "$29/mo unlimited? No — $29/mo, 20 photos. Overage $1.50. Simple enough to expense."

Rule: never send more than 20 DMs/day per account (ban risk). 10/day × 5 days = first paying realtor expected by day 12 at 4% conversion.
