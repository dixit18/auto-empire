# ORG PLAN — 07 Staging Interior AI (org: proptech utility)
Mission: the 30-second stage behind every fast-selling listing.

## Placement
Shelf: real-estate photo tools, between $200/pro staging shoots (slow, booked out) and generic AI restylers (pretty, off-brief). Wedge: agents + Airbnb hosts only — listing photos, not interior dreams. Before/after IS the ad inventory (every share = free distribution).

## Pricing architecture
- Starter $19: 10 photos/mo (try).
- Pro $29 (hero): 20 photos + listing-description writer.
- Team $79: 5 seats for brokerages (land-and-expand motion).
- Overage $1.50/photo (keeps heavy users profitable).
Math: 70×$29 + 5 brokerages ≈ $2.4k MRR; GPU <$0.30/pop keeps >90% margin. Price against $200 shoots, not against free apps.

## Packaging
3 variants per room (Scandi / Neo-Deco / Afrohemian — on-trend per 2026 palette), Zillow/Airbnb description included, share-ready before/after cards with watermark (free tier) / clean (paid).

## Channels (ordered)
1. Free 3-photo trials in agent Facebook groups (10 testimonials). 2. Before/after X + Pinterest flywheel (feeds 05 aesthetics). 3. Brokerage team plans. 4. Contractor white-label (remodel upsell tool).

## 30-60-90 + kill
D30: 10 free trials → 4% convert target. D60: 30 paying. D90: 1 brokerage or stay solo-profitable (this org is allowed to plateau in peace — boring winners compound). Kill rule: share rate <8% = wow-factor failure, fix styles before ads.
