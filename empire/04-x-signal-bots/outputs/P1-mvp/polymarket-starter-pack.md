# Compound rev — Polymarket politics starter pack (Watcher → Signal Chief)
Date: 2026-09-08. Product: $99/mo Telegram alpha. Promise: outcome-moving posts in <1s.

Tracked buckets (start 40 handles, expand to 80):
- Election forecasters (8): model updates move markets hardest — alert on any numeric change.
- SCOTUS watchers (6): opinion days = volatility; pre-write alert templates per case.
- Congressional reporters (12): leadership/schedule news; filter noise with "vote/schedule" keywords.
- Pollsters (8): only A-/B-rated polls; include sample size in alert or bettors ignore you.
- Breaking desks (6): AP, Punchbowl, Axios alerts as confirmation layer.

Alert format (every alert, no exceptions):
`[MARKET] brief what-happened | market slug | why it moves | link | posted Xs ago`
Example: `[POLYMARKET] Fed gov nominee floats 25bp hike | fed-decision | hawkish surprise | link | 3s ago`

Anti-churn rule: max 12 alerts/day. More than that and bettors mute you — silence IS the product. Weekly digest of "alerts that printed" is the retention email.
Risk: prediction markets are regulated in some regions — disclaimer on every message, never "financial advice", always "information only".
