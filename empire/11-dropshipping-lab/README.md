# 11 - Dropshipping Lab (Commerce)
Industry: Commerce / DTC | Model: hero-product + refill subscription | Target: first profitable test in 30 days, $3k/mo at ~25% net

## Doctrine (non-traditional — a master's actual playbook)
Forget "10 winning products" listicles. Those products are winning for the seller of the course, not you — by the time a gadget hits a listicle, 400 stores run the same creative to the same audience and CAC triples. Masters don't hunt products. They hunt **repeatable demonstrations of a felt problem**.

A product only enters this lab if it passes all six gates:
1. **3-second silent demo** — muted autoplay must sell it (thumb stops for motion, not claims).
2. **Felt problem, not spec problem** — embarrassment, fear, disgust, or guilt on camera (tartar, bills, smell).
3. **Built-in LTV** — consumable refill or natural bundle. One-time gadgets = buying revenue every day forever.
4. **$28–60 price** — cheap enough for impulse, expensive enough to survive $10–15 CAC.
5. **≤8-day delivery story** — sourcing edge, not AliExpress 15-day standard (that's where refunds live).
6. **UGC-able by non-actors** — a real customer can film the proof with a phone. If only a studio can demo it, kill it.

## HERO PICK: Senior small-breed dental ritual kit
**Kit:** enzymatic gel (chicken flavor) + dual-head finger brushes (2 sizes) + lick-mat + 30-day "yellow-to-white" tracking card. Wedge: **dogs under 25lbs, age 7+.**
**Why this, thought through like a buyer, not a seller:**
- Pain is vet-verified: ~80% of dogs show dental disease by age 3. Small seniors are the worst — owners SEE brown teeth daily and feel guilty.
- Price anchor is brutal: professional cleaning $300–800 + anesthesia fear for old dogs. Our $34.95 kit vs a $600 vet bill is not a purchase, it's relief.
- Demo is the ad: scrape sound, brown teeth day 1 → white teeth day 14. No acting, no claims — just a phone camera and a calendar card (which we include, so customers generate our content for us).
- LTV is structural: gel runs out monthly → $14.95 refill subscription. Target: 30% of buyers on refill by day 45.
- Competition sells generic chews and green sticks to "all dogs." Nobody owns the senior-small-breed ritual. Narrow wins.
**Unit math:** landed $6.80 (gel white-label + brushes + box) → sell $34.95 (5.1×) → refill $14.95 at 70% margin. CAC ceiling $13. Break-even ROAS ~1.6. Kill any ad set at $25 spend with zero add-to-carts; kill angle at $60 with no purchase.

## Sourcing (the unsexy edge)
No AliExpress retail links. 1688 + sourcing agent, 3 suppliers sampled in parallel, stacked branded box (the box is a billboard in unboxing videos). Start on TikTok Shop fulfillment + ePacket-line for DTC (8-day promise). Graduate to 3PL at 50 orders/day. Never scale past 100/day on agent fulfillment — that's where tracking-number horror stories start.

## Creative engine (video + image strategy)
**Video — 3 formats, 2/day, phone only:**
1. **POV disgust** (0–3s scrape close-up, no talking) → ritual → day-14 reveal. 70% of volume.
2. **Vet-bill shock** — "$647 quote for my 12-year-old's teeth. I tried this first." Invoice prop (redacted) + calendar flip.
3. **Reply-to-comment** — every skeptical comment becomes the next video's script. Comments are free creative direction.
**Hook matrix (rotate, never repeat same hook twice in a row):** "POV: your 11yo's teeth", "Vets hate this $35...", "Day 1 vs Day 14", "Stop brushing (do this)", "The smell test", + 10 more in outputs.
**Images — carousels convert the skeptics video warms up:**
1. Shame → ritual → proof (3 frames). 2. Comparison table: $600 vet clean vs $35 kit. 3. Review screenshots with yellow teeth photos (permission + discount for photo reviews — this is the real moat: 200 real before/afters nobody can copy).

## Testing protocol (30 days, ~$300)
Wk1: samples + 15 videos organic (TikTok + Reels), read comments for winning angle. Wk2: $20/day CBO × 3 angles on Meta + TikTok Shop listings live. Kill rules above, no exceptions, no " Day 4 miracle" spending. Wk3: scale winners +20%/day, whitelist 2 creators at 2× ROAS. Wk4: refill email/SMS flow live, decide hero vs B1 pivot on contribution margin, not revenue.

## Honest note
This is the least passive team: ~$300 test budget + 1–2h/day for 30 days. It becomes semi-passive only when refill revenue covers ad spend. Anyone promising otherwise is selling a course.
