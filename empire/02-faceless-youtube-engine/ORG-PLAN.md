# ORG PLAN — 02 Faceless YouTube Engine (org: media label)
Mission: a faceless label publishing 2× daily in money niches, retention-engineered.

## Placement
Shelf: finance-explainer YouTube, between solo gurus (personality risk) and spam farms (retention death). We are the boring professional middle: researched scripts, clean VO, honest affiliates. Revenue sits on three legs so no single ban kills us: AdSense (~$6.5 CPM finance), affiliates (books/tools/brokers), sponsors at 10k subs.

## Pricing architecture (we sell attention, priced by CPM stack)
- Shorts flywheel: $0 direct, buys subs at ~$0 acquisition (3–5/day).
- Longs (2/week): $6–12 CPM + affiliate links ($20–80 effective CPM on converting videos).
- Sponsors (at 10k): $25–40 CPM flat, 60s mid-roll only.
Math: 12k subs × 100k views/mo × $8 blended = ~$800 + affiliates (2–5× ads) → $2.4–4.5k/mo realistic (TubeMetrics band).

## Packaging
Channel = the package: consistent thumbnail system (3 words + face/circle), series playlists (scams / Etsy money / prediction markets), pinned comment with affiliate stack.

## Channels (ordered)
1. Shorts daily (discovery). 2. Longs weekly (revenue). 3. X threads from scripts (feeds 01). 4. Newsletter repurpose at 5k subs.

## 30-60-90 + kill
D30: 30 Shorts live; kill niche if avg view <500. D60: 2 longs/week + first affiliate $. D90: 5k subs or pivot niche (true crime backup). Kill rule: CTR <4% after 10 videos = packaging problem, fix thumbs before anything else.
