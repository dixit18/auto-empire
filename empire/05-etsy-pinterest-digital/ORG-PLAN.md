# ORG PLAN — 05 Etsy Pinterest Digital (org: digital publishing house)
Mission: forecast Pinterest 6 months early, own the Etsy shelf when demand arrives.

## Placement
Shelf: Etsy digital downloads, between $2 single-page sellers (race to bottom) and $50 course-creators (overkill). We own the $7–35 bundle band: single $6.95 tripwire → $14.95 bundle → $29 mega. Collections (not random listings): ADHD Moms, Poetcore, Midnight Garden — 3 shelves, deep not wide.

## Pricing architecture
- Singles $6.95–8.95 (discovery, 147%-CTR sticker-style economics).
- Bundles $12.95–14.95 (hero margin: 4-in-1 perceived value, 95% margin).
- Mega $29 (everything + lifetime updates).
- Editable Canva premium +$10 (wedding suite $24.95).
Math: 600 sales/yr per niched pack × $13 avg × 3 collections ≈ $2k/mo at ~95% margin. Bundles must stay >60% of revenue — singles are ads that pay.

## Packaging
Lifestyle mockups only (marble desk, candle scenes — never flat PDF screenshots), Rich Pins, "print forever" framing, free sample page as list-builder → $197 master upsell email.

## Channels (ordered)
1. Pinterest 2–3 pins/day, 60–90 days BEFORE season (Christmas by Oct, wedding by Jan). 2. Etsy SEO long-tails. 3. Email free→bundle→master ladder. 4. TikTok Shop printables at 1k followers.

## 30-60-90 + kill
D30: 10 listings, 50 pins; kill any listing <3% CTR after 1k impressions (mockup first, title second). D60: 1 bundle/live collection winning → double it. D90: $500/mo or rotate collection (keep winners, kill losers — publishers prune).
