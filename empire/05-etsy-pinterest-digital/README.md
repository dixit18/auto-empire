# 05 - Etsy Pinterest Digital Empire (E-commerce Digital)
Industry: E-commerce Digital | Model: $7-49/listing, bundles $12-35 | Target: $2k/mo passive

## Business
Problem: Generic planners saturated. Buyers want identity + specific solution.
Solution: 3 collections aligned to Pinterest Predicts 2026 (pin 60-90 days EARLY):
A) ADHD Moms Self-Care Bundle $7-19 (Pinterest powerhouse, 32/40 score)
B) Poetcore Stationery + Pen Pal Kit $12-29 (Poet +175%, snail mail +110%, wax seals)
C) Midnight Garden Wedding Suite $19-49 (dark florals, editable Canva)
ICP: US women 22-44 planning on Pinterest.
Math: Single $3-5, bundle $15-25, mega $35. 600 sales/yr per niched pack realistic. 95% margin.
Moat: Trend forecasting outside Etsy (Pinterest, luxury, TikTok) 6-8mo before Etsy. Collage pins 2x clicks.

## TEAM
**MASTER: Trend Director**
Prompt: "You own Etsy SEO + Pinterest. Coordinate Forecaster, Designer, SEOlister. No generic. 3 collections only. Every product Pinterest-worthy."

**A1 - Forecaster:** Checks trends.pinterest.com US weekly, TikTok hashtags, eRank. Outputs: color (Cool Blue, Plum Noir, Jade, Wasabi, Persimmon) + aesthetic + keywords.
**A2 - Designer:** Canva/Figma templates: imperfect hand-drawn stripes, vintage frames, gummy textures. Lifestyle mockups (marble desk + gold pen), not flat PDF.
**A3 - SEOlister:** Titles long-tail ("ADHD daily planner undated printable moms"), 13 tags, buyer-focused description, Rich Pins.
**A4 - Pinner:** 3-5 pins per product, text overlay with keywords, schedule Tailwind 2-3/day. Tracks impressions -> clicks -> sales.

Handoff: Forecaster (trend) -> Designer (mockup) -> SEOlister (live) -> Pinner (traffic loop).

## PHASES
P0 (Wk1): Pick 1 collection (ADHD moms). Create 5-10 listings, not 100.
P1 (Wk2-4): Publish + 5 pins each. Live by Oct for Christmas, July for Halloween, Jan-May for wedding.
P2 (Mo2-3): Double winners into bundles ($18). Add editable wedding premium $25.
P3 (Mo4+): Expand to 2nd collection, email list with free sample -> upsell $197 master.

KPIs: CTR (stickers benchmark 147%), conversion, avg order ($12+ via bundles), pin lifespan (months).
First $100: 10 x $12 bundle = launch to mom Facebook groups + Pinterest.
