# ORG PLAN — 10 Micro-Course Factory (org: tiny school)
Mission: one problem, ninety minutes, templates included — courses people finish.

## Placement
Shelf: online learning, between free YouTube (unfinished, unsequenced) and $500 cohorts (overkill for one skill). Wedge: micro-outcomes with completion baked in (90 mins + Notion + checklist). First school: "Pinterest SEO for Etsy" — sells to our own empire's audience before anyone else's.

## Pricing architecture
- Micro-course $29 (hero: 90 mins + templates; pre-sell $19 × 10 before filming).
- Template pack $35 standalone (catches non-learners).
- Community $19/mo (office hours + FAQ bot — retention + upsell surface for 06 agents).
- Workshop $149/seat B2B (schools/agencies, quarterly).
Math: 100×$29 + 50×$19 = $3.8k launch + MRR tail. Completion >40% is the marketed metric (industry ~5–15%).

## Packaging
6×15-min lessons, Notion dashboard, checklists, free sample pack (lead magnet) → $197 master upsell. Paywalled course-bot ($39/mo flavor of 06) as TA.

## Channels (ordered)
1. Pre-sell to existing audiences (X + 05 buyers). 2. Sample-pack SEO ("AI tools for accountants/teachers" style). 3. Gumroad discovery. 4. B2B workshops.

## 30-60-90 + kill
D30: 10 pre-sales or the topic dies before filming (rule, no exceptions). D60: filmed + launched, 100 buyers. D90: community 50 or course stands alone. Kill rule: completion <25% = packaging failure (shorten, don't market harder).
