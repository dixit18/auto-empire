Evidence: S26 batch analyses (236 cos), Fall 2026 RFS (13 asks), funding trail (TechCrunch), PG essays, YC Startup School. Full receipts: IDEAS-YC-2026.md, PROBLEMS.md, WHITESPACE.md.

## 10 derivations
1. **Agents became the customer (B2A).** Cards, identity, computers, filesystems, logs, integrations, spend attribution, control planes — the exact stack a company assembles when hiring a human. Build plumbing, not promises.
2. **Measure/fix/defend beats build.** Evals, sandboxes, red-teaming, analytics everywhere = industrial maturity. Picks-and-shovels get funded when the app layer crowds.
3. **Memory is settled fact, deployment is open.** Mem0/Supermemory/Letta prove demand; ALL are cloud meters. Local-first + readable + fixed-cost is unclaimed at indie scale.
4. **Multiplayer is asked for twice** (S26 Dock/Mosaic/Glen + Fall RFS shared sessions) — but everyone prices enterprise. Tiny-team wedge open.
5. **Agent-run firms > agent tools.** Accounting/law/ERP startups sell the OUTCOME. Lesson: our services (08 autopilot, 03 DFY) outrank our tools wherever trust allows.
6. **Median team is 2 people.** Tiny crews shipping infra validates our agent-crew model — leverage beats headcount now.
7. **Physical/industrial 23% is not ours.** No hardware, no wet labs. Take only the lesson (data layers, eval discipline).
8. **Wells win: narrow + deep with a path out.** Our wells: 05 shelves, 11 guilt+anchor, 12 builders-like-us. Everything else is search.
9. **Tar pits named:** generic wrappers, broad courses, "best AI" claims. 06 lives only as Etsy-vertical.
10. **Burn boats at $1k.** Portfolio search is deliberate and temporary; first real $1k concentrates 80% of crew.
