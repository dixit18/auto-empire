# 08 - LocalBiz Autopilot (Local Services Automation)
Industry: Local Services | Model: $199-499/mo retainer + $750-2500 setup | Target: $5k from 10 clients

## Business
Problem: Dentists, salons, contractors miss calls, get bad reviews, slow inbox.
Solution: One offer: "Missed-call textback in 60s + AI review responder in your voice + lead summary". Not generic consulting - clear deliverable.
Proof: IG DM automation agencies $3k-15k/mo. Chatbot setup easiest B2B sell (measurable: fewer manual tasks).
ICP: 5-50 employee local biz, owner-operator, already spending on ads.
Math: 10 x $399 = $3990 + setups. Low churn if you save 1 job/mo.
Moat: Human judgment + AI speed. Can't be faked with pure AI replies - need approval loop.

## TEAM
**MASTER: Ops Manager**
Prompt: "You protect reputation. Coordinate Inbox, Reviewer, Booker. Never auto-post angry review. Draft -> approve -> send."

**A1 - InboxAgent:** Summarizes new inquiries (web, FB, missed call), drafts reply, routes hot leads to owner SMS.
**A2 - Reviewer:** Responds on-brand, de-escalating, SEO keywords. Flags 1-star for human.
**A3 - Booker:** Fills calendar (Calendly/GoHighLevel), sends reminders, reduces no-shows.
**A4 - Reporter:** Weekly ROI sheet: leads caught, reviews won, hours saved.

## PHASES
P0: Pick 1 niche (e.g., salons). Offer "5 SEO product descriptions in 24h" style narrow: "Free 7-day missed-call rescue trial".
P1: GoHighLevel + OpenAI + Twilio. 2 beta clients free for testimonial.
P2: $199/mo starter, $499 pro. Outreach via cold + local FB groups + Reddit miner (#03 feeds this).
P3: Productize: setup fee + playbook + referral (1 free month).

KPIs: Response <60s, review rating lift, booked calls, churn <5%.
First $100: 1 setup audit $100.
